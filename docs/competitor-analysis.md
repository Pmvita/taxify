# Competitor analysis (Taxify)

Taxify sits between CRA filing products and general accounting — a transparent **estimate** calculator, not a filer.

| Product | Strengths | Gap Taxify exploits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TurboTax Canada | Guided return, CRA auto-fill, paid expert tiers | Filing-first; math is opaque; not a free forever province switcher |
| Keeper Tax | Expense tracking, quarterly estimates | US / IRS oriented |
| Wave | Free Canadian accounting (invoicing, receipts) | Not a take-home / bracket calculator |
| Xero / FreshBooks / QuickBooks | Books, payroll, accountant workflows | Management reports, not live province-aware take-home math |

**Product bet:** transparent federal + provincial brackets, person-type switching, and province compare — client-side, free for estimates, no NETFILE.
